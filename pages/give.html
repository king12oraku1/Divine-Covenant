Why We Give

Giving is an Act of Worship

Giving is not just a financial transaction — it is an act of worship and trust. When we give, we declare that God is our provider and that His kingdom matters more than our own comfort.

Every gift to Divine Covenant RCCG — whether a tithe, offering or special gift — goes directly toward supporting our ministries, reaching people with the gospel, caring for our community and building the house of God.

"Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver." 2 Corinthians 9:7
