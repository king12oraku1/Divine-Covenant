First Time?

Plan Your Visit

We want your first visit to be a great experience. Here's what to expect when you join us on a Sunday:

👋

Warm Welcome

Our welcome team will be at the door to greet you, answer any questions and help you find your seat.

🎵

Vibrant Worship

Expect powerful, Spirit-led worship that creates an atmosphere for genuine encounter with God.

📖

Relevant Teaching

Our messages are grounded in the Bible and practically applied to everyday life.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family Friendly

We have dedicated children's provision during the service so the whole family can be catered for.

☕

Fellowship

Stay after the service to meet people and enjoy refreshments together. Community is at the heart of who we are.

👕

Come As You Are

There is no dress code and no expectation. Just come as you are — you are welcome exactly as you are.
